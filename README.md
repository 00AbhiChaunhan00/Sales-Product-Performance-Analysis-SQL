## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales and product performance data using **MySQL** to answer real-world business questions.

The analysis covers **markets, customers, products, segments, sales channels, manufacturing costs, discounts, and fiscal-year performance**.

The objective is to transform raw business data into actionable insights that can support **sales strategy, product planning, customer targeting, and channel optimization**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project answers the following key business questions:

1. Identify the markets where **Atliq Exclusive** operates in the APAC region.
2. Calculate the percentage increase in unique products from **2020 to 2021**.
3. Analyze unique product counts across different segments.
4. Identify the segment with the highest increase in unique products.
5. Identify products with the highest and lowest manufacturing costs.
6. Find the Top 5 Indian customers with the highest average pre-invoice discount percentage in FY2021.
7. Analyze monthly gross sales for **Atliq Exclusive**.
8. Identify the quarter with the highest total sold quantity in 2020.
9. Identify the sales channel contributing the highest gross sales in FY2021.
10. Find the Top 3 products in each division based on total sold quantity in FY2021.

# 🔍 Analysis Performed

## 1. APAC Market Analysis

Identified all markets where **Atliq Exclusive** operates within the **APAC region**.

### Business Purpose

Helps understand the company's geographical presence and market coverage.

---

## 2. Unique Product Growth

Compared the number of unique products available in:

- FY2020
- FY2021

Calculated the percentage change to measure product portfolio growth.

### Business Purpose

Helps identify whether the company expanded its product portfolio over time.

---

## 3. Segment-wise Product Analysis

Calculated the number of unique products available in each segment and sorted segments by product count.

### Business Purpose

Helps identify segments with the largest product portfolios.

---

## 4. Segment Product Growth

Compared product counts for each segment between **2020 and 2021**.

### Business Purpose

Identifies which segment experienced the highest product expansion.

---

## 5. Manufacturing Cost Analysis

Identified products with:

- Highest manufacturing cost
- Lowest manufacturing cost

### Business Purpose

Helps understand cost variation across products and identify products that may require further cost analysis.

---

## 6. Customer Discount Analysis

Identified the **Top 5 customers in India** based on their average pre-invoice discount percentage during FY2021.

### Business Purpose

Helps evaluate customer discount patterns and understand where higher discounts are being offered.

---

## 7. Monthly Gross Sales Analysis

Analyzed monthly gross sales generated by **Atliq Exclusive**.

### Business Purpose

Helps identify:

- High-performing months
- Low-performing months
- Sales trends
- Seasonal patterns

---

## 8. Quarterly Sold Quantity Analysis

Compared total sold quantity across quarters in 2020.

### Business Purpose

Helps identify the quarter with the strongest sales volume.

---

## 9. Channel-wise Gross Sales Contribution

Calculated gross sales for each sales channel during FY2021 along with its percentage contribution to total gross sales.

### Business Purpose

Helps identify the most important sales channels and understand their contribution to overall revenue.

---

## 10. Top 3 Products by Division

Used SQL ranking techniques to identify the **Top 3 products within each division** based on total sold quantity during FY2021.

### Business Purpose

Helps identify the best-performing products within each business division.
